The big idea

Your life's not too full. It's built in the wrong order.

God never let His people build first and ask for the plan later. Noah got the dimensions before he cut a single board. Moses saw the pattern on the mountain before he built the tabernacle. Solomon got the plans before he laid a stone.

Most of us have flipped it. We build first, then ask God to bless what we put together in a panic.

Here's the catch: whatever you build on hustle takes hustle to keep running. So instead of carrying you, it drains you. Your wife gets what's left. So does God.

A business running on hustle instead of favor is a tent dressed up as a house.

This isn't about ranking faith, family and business in the right order on a list. You already know faith matters more than money. That's never been the problem.

The problem is nobody ever gave you a system to run it that way.

So you put a new operating system under each one: your faith, your family and your finances. And you put men around you who hold you to it.
