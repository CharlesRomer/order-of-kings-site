01

Somewhere along the way, the business started costing you more than it should.

Maybe it's growing and you're more tired than you've ever been. Maybe it's stalled and you can't work out why. Either way, your wife is getting what's left of you. Your kids are getting a tired dad. And God is getting whatever you can squeeze in before the phone starts going off.

You've tried to fix it. You worked harder. You hired someone. You told yourself you'd slow down after this season. It's been three seasons.

You worked harder→Your family got less of you
You hired someone→It still runs through you
You went on the marriage retreat→The business fell behind
You tried cutting back→It felt like walking away from the call
"Ye looked for much, and, lo, it came to little." — Haggai 1:9

Every fix pulled from somewhere else. That's why nothing stuck.

02

It's not a time problem.
It's not an effort problem.
It's an order problem.

Your life has been designed backwards.

DESIGNED BACKWARDS

You've been running your life like the business is the root and everything else grows off it. So your family gets the leftovers, and God gets what's left after that.

God's order runs the other way. Faith is the root. Your family is your first assignment. The business is the fruit, and it grows better there, not worse. Put it back in order and you stop having to choose between them.
