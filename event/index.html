Sound familiar?

You've worked hard to build a business. Somewhere along the way it started costing you more than it should.

Maybe the business is growing and you're more tired than ever. Maybe it's stalled and you can't figure out why. Either way, your wife's getting less of you than she should, and God's getting whatever's left at the end of the day.

You've probably tried to fix it. Worked harder. Hired someone. Told yourself you'd slow down after this season. It didn't change much.

That's because the problem was never effort. Your life's been put together in the wrong order.

What business owners told us on calls, in their own words.

The big idea

It's not a time problem. It's an order problem.

Every fix you've tried pulled from somewhere else. The business coach took more time from your family. The marriage retreat put the business behind. Cutting back felt like walking away from the call. It's a seesaw, and the only way off is to fix the order, not the parts.
